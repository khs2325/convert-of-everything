Sprite to Aseprite Converter

Rebuild editable Aseprite timelines from supported PNG sequences, spritesheets, animated images, and raster project files.

Artwork is parsed and converted in your browser. It is not uploaded to a conversion server. Flat images can provide frames, but they cannot recover original editor layers.

Choose the right input format, read how browser-local conversion works, or open troubleshooting. The interactive converter loads when JavaScript is available.
